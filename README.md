# Cybersecurity Research and Methodology
This repository is a centralized hub for my independent cybersecurity research and technical deep dives. My focus is on the intersection of regulatory compliance and proactive defense. This is a growing resource that documents my methodology as I continue to explore the threat landscape.

---

## Current Research Areas
### PCI DSS 4.0 and 4.0.1 Handbook
A comprehensive technical guide to the transition from PCI DSS v3.2.1 to v4.0. This research covers the operational impact on the SOC, continuous compliance strategies, and the business risks associated with the new version 4.0 requirements.

### Command and Control (C2) Framework Analysis
An ongoing exploration of adversary emulation tools and C2 frameworks. This research focuses on identifying unique traffic patterns and developing detection logic for common C2 communication channels.

---

## Research Objectives
*   Actionable Defense: Translating theoretical research into practical security controls and detection signatures.
*   Methodological Rigor: Documenting the process behind the research to ensure findings are repeatable and verifiable.
*   Industry Alignment: Staying current with global compliance standards and emerging adversary tactics.
